# Changelog

## v0.8 — Party Rumble: Showdown
- Made the player's character unmistakable: bright cyan body, floating gold crown marker and glowing gold ring at the feet.
- HÚC now automatically targets the nearest opponent within range and applies a strong outward knockback impulse.
- Opponents are eliminated when knocked beyond the arena boundary; remaining-player count and rank update.
- Added action cooldown indicators so HÚC and KHIÊN feedback is visible.
- Shortened the round to 60 seconds and made the arena shrink sooner for a faster match.
- Preserved all previous versions unchanged.

## v0.7 — Party Rumble
- First circular 3D arena prototype with seven AI opponents, dash, shield, rotating sweeper and shrinking arena.
- Added mobile virtual joystick.

## v0.6 — Party Dash
- Added POMI plush-like mascot, collectible stars, combo, shield and magnet power-ups.
